# Logos map

Logos is the dictionary of life: its sections are social organization, emotion, nature and the senses, and its grammar is how they combine.

*(John, 2026-11-17)* "We are finally needing to fully flesh out logos as the dictionary of life through its grammar with sounds and visions, and the analog of nature (science) and social organization."

- **Social organization — built, text only.** The most developed section; the field operators mostly read here.
- Speech acts, Grice's maxims, authority, transmission and utility: `logos/logos_schema_v01.json`, run by the matching `*_operator.py` files.
- Douglas grid/group and Dunbar's layers (5, 15, 150 ...): `logos_schema_v01.json` (`social_field`) and `logos_social_v01.json`.
- Scale, density, persistence and memory channel: `logos/logos_combined_v01.json` (structural axes), run by `structural_operator.py`.
- Bonds (Bowlby, Bowen, Boss, Sbarra & Hazan): `bond_operator.py`; found in family courts but evolutionary, so core logos.
- **Emotion and body language — present but thin.** Felt is a real face of logos; the code reaches it only through words.
- `resonance` in `logos_schema_v01.json` names the emotional field an act carries; `resonance_operator.py` applies it.
- The left pass in `vivify.py` asks for "felt meaning" (`emotional_truth`, `therapeutic_potential`): affect only, not the whole left.
- `logos_schema_v01.json` (`what_this_is_not`) excludes "the non-verbal, silent, affective layer" and hands it to social neurology.
- Social neurology is the old site's theoretical framework (`robolawyer-tm.github.io/index.html`, #neurology), not yet an operator.
- **Nature (science) — empty.** No file holds logos as the evolved analog people built of the natural world.
- Candidate entries: seasons, kinship with animals, cause and effect, number and measure; all pre-digital ways of modelling nature.
- This is the section that connects to the digital right: science is where the analog was formalized into synthesis.
- **The senses (sound, vision, gesture) — empty.** Everything read so far is text: court opinions and session transcripts.
- `language_mode` (indexical through formalized) in `logos_combined_v01.json` is the only axis that orders forms of expression.
- `NOTATION_VISION.md` is about visual notation for process flows, a tool, not logos itself.
- Song, film, image and body language each carry logos that a transcript drops; they are the "sounds and visions".
- **Grammar — open.** How the sections combine is the least defined part and the most important.
- The TCP/IP-on-Maslow image (one event registers at every level, differently at each) is a grammar intuition, deliberately not codified.
- `_relationship` in `logos_combined_v01.json` holds the open question of how function and structure meet: "inference store decides".
- Empathy is the glue that built logos (2026-10-23): a candidate for the grammar's binding rule, not a section.
- **Where it goes next is undecided.** No section is a task; the map shows what exists and where it is empty.
- Reading order for a newcomer: social organization (built), then emotion (thin), then the two empty sections.
