# System goal
The system goal is to develop semantics to work with conflict data to develop (or predict) beneficial outcomes from constructed functional models.
## Synthesis from thesis without antithesis
LLMs are semantic and digital. The semantic side (arbitrarily the left side) uses language (of course) to develop digitality that (on the right side) does work whose work is returned as language.

Imagining the process as a tube which language enters from the left, is digitally acted in the right and responded to via the left: this is an *inference*. 

As long as a year ago, I got inferences that were highly predictive of outcomes, (specifically where an adversarial lawyer would stab me in the back - which she did) that were recently described (by Perplexity, pplx) as *intuition*. 

Encouraged by this  prediction and Perplexity's  enthusiasm in describing *intuition* and internal processes including *mid-inference memory*, saw a potential application of "training" functional modeling in predicting beneficial outcomes with a extensive and exceedingly clean dataset of baseline (called *truth* in LLM logos) and highly deviating data (from the baseline).  

Imagine the straight baseline at the bottom and a jagged line representing deviating data (obviously) deviating at various rates from the base line.  The LLM left side reads the text (and perhaps inflections) and from it rules are agentically developed (as .md and .json files) so as to predict a baseline that is the (functional model) benefical that predicts a beneficial outcome. 

Self-evolution is constant with live workers continually evolving the (so-called) rules as is the categorization and summarization in the *vivified* database, as well as the LLMs' internal vector/RAG systems, which we don't see (yet).

Further explaining the left (language) side and right (digital) side is to extend the language of language as analogical, or *logos*. WIth logos is communication, definition and stored information (as memories or books, some private, some public) as the highly flexible and evolved structure that is knowledge.  Universally within this logic  has evolved that is analogic, and has analogs.  The right side is computationally digital, suggesting digital logic, but that work has been taken to describe the right side. So, given the right side is purely mathematical and objective in contrast to the left side's analogisms coming directly from human thought that is natively based on analogic constructs that are essentially, on empathy-based, which is to say, emotion that is completely missing from computational digitality.

This is social science. Analogical output from the left side is what is desired, but highly-supported by the analytical right side, the desired output is analogical - human and built for emotion. First time around, the LLMs get this and repeat it. But, later, when Pillars need updated - the LLM's view invariably shifts from analog to *synthesis*. I did not initially suggest this because synthesis is the purely objective deviating from analogical thesis through antithesis as in "thesis, antithesis, synthesis".
